How to play
  • Wait for the Alto to boot to the > prompt; the page types trek for you automatically.
  • Everything is mouse-driven: click the outer ring of the Direction circle to steer, the Accel bar to thrust (click its bottom to cut thrust), the Fire boxes to shoot, and next to your ship in the big scanner to raise shields (left-click up, right-click down).
  • Click the top-left scanner for the Galaxy map (right-click returns).
  • Typing broadcasts a chat message to all players. ESC B deploys a base, ESC Q quits.
  • Don't drift idle — gravity pulls you into stars and wrecks your ship.
About

Alto Trek was written by Gene Ball and Rick Rashid at the University of Rochester in the late 1970s for the Xerox Alto — one of the first networked multiplayer games ever made. There is no server-side game logic: exactly as in 1978, every ship broadcasts its state onto the shared (now virtual) Ethernet.

Emulation by ContrAltoJS (AGPL-3.0), a JavaScript port of the Living Computer Museum's ContrAlto. Source for this site (including its WebSocket networking changes): github.com/erickball/alto-trek.
